Both videos use the same throw, starting pose, cameras and physics, and each shows the complete nine second simulation. Switching controllers restarts the play. This is a selected example, not a success-rate estimate.

PPO adjusts fourteen arm targets and six native hand groups using color and depth, IMU and encoders. Locomotion and the nominal grasp are pretrained or scripted. Checkpoint 19,456 at half action gain was selected after screening. These CPU trials reproduce exactly in a fresh Linux environment.
